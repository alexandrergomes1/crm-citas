# Legal and Privacy

This folder stores legal, privacy, consent and compliance risk documentation.

These documents are not final legal text. They are product and architecture guidance for implementation, risk review and later legal validation.

## Active documents

- `privacy-consent-risk-register.md`: RGPD/GDPR, LOPDGDD, LGPD, consent purposes, image authorization, anamnese, messaging and payment/deposit risks.

## Rules

- Do not implement final privacy text directly from these documents without jurisdiction validation.
- Do not mix clinical/anamnese consent, public image use, marketing and payment consent into a single checkbox.
- Do not treat archived audits as legal basis.
- Any payment, messaging provider or review integration must be checked for privacy, processor and jurisdiction impact before implementation.
